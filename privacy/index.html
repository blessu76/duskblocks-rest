Privacy Policy

2026-11-16

In short: the Dusk Blocks app collects no personal information and contains no analytics or ads. This website is a static site with no analytics and no cookies, and it asks for no name, email or account.

Effective date: 2026-11-14 · Operator: Davy Kim (ipe Studio)

1. Information we collect

Dusk Blocks ("the App") is a mobile game operated by an individual developer, Davy Kim (ipe Studio). The App has no accounts or sign-in, and it does not collect or send to any server any personal information such as your name, email, contact details, location or device identifiers.

Your game progress, scores and settings are stored on your device and are deleted from it when you delete the App. If you use iCloud sync (see below), they are also kept in your own iCloud. The game works without an internet connection.

The App contains no advertising, analytics or tracking SDKs.

iCloud sync (iOS). So that you can keep playing on another device signed in with the same Apple ID, the iOS App keeps your game progress in your own iCloud and syncs it between your devices. This data is kept in your iCloud account and is not sent to us or to any server we run; we cannot see it. If you turn iCloud off for the App (or for your device), your progress is kept on that device only.

2. The website

This website (duskblocks.ipe.rest) is a static site. It uses no analytics tools and sets no cookies. If you pick a language, your browser may keep that choice on your own device only; it is never sent to us. Pages use your device's own fonts. The site is hosted on GitHub Pages, which may process ordinary web request information (such as your IP address) under GitHub's own policies.

3. Third parties

We do not sell your information and do not share it with anyone. When the App is released, downloads will be handled by the app stores under their own privacy policies.

4. Questions and your rights

To request access, correction, deletion or suspension of processing of your personal information, or to ask any question, write to ipe.haven@gmail.com. Because the App and the website collect no personal information, we usually hold nothing to look up; email correspondence we can locate and delete.

5. Children

The App is not directed to children under 16 (or the minimum age in your region), and the App collects no personal information from any user.

If the App or the website later adds accounts or online features that change what is collected, we will update this policy first and change the effective date.
